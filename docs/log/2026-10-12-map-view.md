# The Map view: a place field, Apple's geocoder and the view (12 Oct 2026, Q46, G13)

Branch `feat/map-view`, made from `feat/ui-audit-fixes` at 2f0a57c so PR #5 stays window-only.
Commits 4208326 (core), 93ce1dd (window), 86044dd (the review's fixes and a journey), then the
clustering and size fix. Built to `design/map-view-plan.md` and the owner's answers there.

## What was built

- **Core: a `place` field kind** (`world/collections.py`).
  - A value is `{label, lat, lon, precision, source, estimated}`. Text written to a place field
    becomes `{label}` and is looked up before the transaction opens. A point given with its value
    is kept as given.
  - Words the table has placed already keep their point, so a person's own point isn't replaced
    by an estimate and a re-sync asks Apple nothing.
  - A place filters, searches and exports (CSV, Excel) as its words.
  - `to_place` turns a text field into a place field in one transaction: it keeps versions and
    provenance, and counts located, not found and couldn't be looked up now.
- **Apple's geocoder** (`connectors/places.py`): CoreLocation through pyobjc, in a child process
  that spins its own run loop.
  - About 0.2 s a lookup; six cities in 1.3 s on the check world.
  - A result counts only when one of its names is in what was asked.
  - **Differs from the plan:** it runs in the core like EventKit, not through a Tauri plugin.
    Nothing under `desktop/src-tauri/` changed.
- **Alpha proposes it:** the `collection_propose_place` tool writes a proposal to Needs you. The
  person's yes (`/api/proposals/{id}/decide`) makes the change, then the journal records it.
- **Window: Map in the View dropdown** (`modules/views/MapView.tsx`).
  - Natural Earth's countries at 1:50m through d3-geo and world-atlas, offline, in greys.
  - One accent dot per record; a looked-up place is hollow and a given one filled.
  - Dots near on screen merge into a numbered circle that a click zooms into. Records at one
    point (a city's) list in the card instead.
  - A click shows the card (title, two fields, Open page); a double-click opens the page.
  - The wheel, a drag and ZoomControls move the map. Arrow keys walk the dots with one tab stop,
    Enter selects and then opens, and Escape closes the card.
  - "N records have no place; M couldn't be found", with Show them, which opens them in the Table.
  - Map is disabled without a place field: "Map needs a place field. Ask Alpha in the panel to
    add one."
  - A place shows and edits as its words everywhere, with the Estimated chip in its cell.
- **Rulebook:** §6 view types.

## The review

An agent that did not build reviewed the diff and found 12 things. All were fixed except where
noted:
- Excel export crashed on a place;
- a re-write replaced a person's point;
- an unchanged place edit still wrote;
- records in one city could never be reached;
- History showed "[object Object]";
- the yes was journalled before the change;
- `to_place` read its schema outside its transaction;
- a failed lookup was counted as not found;
- two-letter country codes matched words like "in";
- filters read a place as JSON;
- MapView's O(n²) on drag, wheel speed, resize and Escape;
- the tool returned an error instead of raising.

Not built: re-locating the places that couldn't be looked up, other than by editing them, and a
person dropping a point on the map.
