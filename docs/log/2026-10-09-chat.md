**The reply as it is written** (`claude_cli`: `--include-partial-messages`, `_watch` on
`stream_event` deltas, `Live.add_text`, `partial` in a turn's `live`; the window polls a
running turn twice a second). The text of the reply shows in the working bubble as the model
writes it, under "Writing", with the steps so far as a short list that ticks off ("Added a row
to Food Log." with a check) and "all N steps" when there are more than three. Text written
before a step was a thought, not the reply: it goes back to the headline and the reply starts
afresh after the step. The view follows the newest words unless the person scrolled up. The
first words' time is kept with the turn (`first_text_ms`) and `alpha turns` prints it as
"first s", so the wait is measured, not felt.

**A question in prose becomes a card, by mechanism** (`runtime/asking.py`, hooked after the
reply in `turn.ask`). When a reply ends with a question and the model made no card and
proposed no plan or action in the same turn, the core makes the `asked` entry itself. Options
come from the reply when it listed them just before the question (2 to 4 list items); when
the question is a choice ("which", "or", "do you want", "should I") with no list, the System
One seam writes 2 to 4 short ones from the reply in about two seconds; an open question (a
name, a folder, a number) gets a card with no options, so the answer still goes straight back
into the conversation. Several questions at once (a plan's numbered ones) stay prose. The
person's next words answer the card as before. The rule to the model stands; it now says a
question left in prose becomes a card anyway, with poorer options than its own.

**The Assistant page** (`assistant/AssistantPage.tsx`, the rail item above Intelligence,
`#/assistant` and `#/assistant/<conversation>`, ⌘K). The conversation at full width (860 px
of text, centred), with every conversation Alpha has had across modules down the left, newest
first, with its scope, its state and its last line; New starts one. The side panel and its
Ask Alpha button step aside while the page is open. The panel and the page are one component
(`Conversation`, `layout` "panel" or "page"), so the cards, the streamed reply and the
composer are the same in both.

**A question followed by a short remark, and an offered list.** Two shapes the first real
turns showed that "?" at the end misses: "…or a salad bowl? The calories differ a lot between
the two." (the question with one short remark after it) and "If you want something more
specific, tell me which: • only certain listings… • a different way to hear about it" (an
offer with the choices as a list, no question mark). Both become cards; the list's items are
shortened to their first clause as the options. A reply that merely says "let me know if…"
stays prose: it is not a question. The reply bubble shows without the question its card
carries, so the person reads it once; a reply that was only the question shows as the card.
