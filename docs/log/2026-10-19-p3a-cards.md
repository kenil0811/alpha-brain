## What was built

### The core
- **`world/cards.py` and the `cards` table.** One row per card, whatever it asks.
  - Its kind: question, permission, pick, reminder, check, routing, signin, proposal,
    instruction, offer, action or workflow.
  - Where it came from: the drafter, thread, module, turn, run, the task the run served, and the
    action, page or task it is about.
  - What it rests on: the `saw` lines of its run or turn, read by code.
  - Its state: waiting, answered, yes, edited, no, allowed, skipped or withdrawn. Also the
    answer, the reason, who decided, when, and its twins.
- **Written by the journal itself.** `Journal.append` writes the card in the same transaction
  as every `asked`, `proposed` and `answered` line. Every producer, today's and later ones, is
  covered, and so is every way of closing, including a bare `answered` line.
  - An answer's state comes from the line's own keys: `closed`, `accept`, `allowed`,
    `edited`, `reason`.
  - For a removal's yes and Scout's pick, the code-written options are matched (the first
    option is the yes). The words are never read for meaning (Q78).
- **One waiting card per identical step.** The key is the drafter, the target and the
  content: for a reminder its task, for a check its row and field, for an action its action.
  - A twin is not written: the waiting card's id comes back, and the card takes the newer turn.
  - A removal's yes is never a twin: each turn waits on its own.
- **`open_asks` reads the waiting cards**, with the card's turn. So the next message answers a
  twin where it was asked last.
- **Needs you is the waiting cards**, oldest first. Its old 50-line window is gone.
  - An action's card shows while its action is proposed; a page's while it is drafted.
  - Each item carries `by` and `from`: who drafted it, the run and when it began, the task, and
    what it rests on. Home's question card used to drop `by`.
- **A card closed without a turn settles its item.** When nothing waits in a thread any more,
  its item goes from `needs_you` to `done`.
- **A No can carry one of six reasons:** Wrong place, Duplicate, Not needed, Bad timing,
  Wrong recipient, Something else.
  - It is accepted on a proposal, an action's decline, a page's decline and an answer, and
    checked against the six.
  - The drafting agent's next run gets "What the person said no to, and why" once
    (`told_at`).
- **Removal withdraws, never deletes:** a module, a conversation, a connection (its
  procedures' actions and its readers' sign-ins) and an agent.
  - The waiting cards of what is removed turn withdrawn; the lines stay in the journal.
  - At start, the waiting cards of removed modules, gone agents and gone threads are withdrawn.
- **A world from before** gets its cards once, from its own journal, in order, with the
  answers' times (`backfill`). His copy made 11; none waited.
- **A task with a waiting card says so.** The tasks table gains `waiting` ("Waiting for your
  yes"), kept by code after the line's transaction, and a saved list "Waiting for my yes" (added
  once to a table made before).
- **Drafters kept:** `propose` and `instruction_propose` journal as whoever uses the tools.
- **Routes:**
  - `GET /api/decisions`: every card, newest first;
  - `GET /api/tasks/{id}/cards`;
  - `reason` on proposals, answers, an action's decline and a page's decline.
- **A page's yes or no decides its card** (`Workflows._move`).

- **Every card says where it came from:**
