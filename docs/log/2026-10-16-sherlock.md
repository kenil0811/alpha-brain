## What was built

- **`runtime/agents/sherlock.py`** (the fox): `look` since its cursor finds a drafted page
  (`proposed` with `data.workflow`) and an interview begun in a conversation with no page yet
  (Alpha asked cards there). `judge_look` is one small run (`sherlock_look.md`): none, a quick
  look at the open parts, or a full look, with the kind of work named; recorded as Sherlock's
  decision; the person's words win by code ("no research", "just build it", or Skip earlier:
  a binding decision of theirs with `no_research`). `look_into` is one turn under its name in
  its own thread (`sherlock_research.md`: the five lenses, reads only, pages through existing
  connections, never a new sign-in; the method the model's; the answer a JSON shape), journaled
  as "Sherlock looks into how others do …" with its live step and Stop. `check_findings` is
  the code around what comes back: every suggestion needs a source that resolves (`url_alive`,
  by code) and a reason that shares a real word with the page (a step, a thing kept, a setting,
  the purpose), else it is dropped with its why; at most `[sherlock] most_suggestions = 5`;
  never one the person skipped before on that page; questions with 2–4 options and its pick
  first.
- **Where it lands** (`land`): a findings page per look, written by code from the JSON (per
  lens, worth having with sources, worth asking, does not apply, left out by the checks) at
  `topic:<slug>` so the Knowledge tab lists it; the suggestions onto the drafted page as a new
  version marked Sherlock's (`Workflows.add_suggestions`, `by`/`by_name` on a suggestion, the
  card shows a badge and the section becomes "Suggestions"); a proposal card when the person
  already said yes ("Sherlock found 2 things worth adding to …", its yes a turn); findings kept
  (`sherlock_findings` on a `did`) when the page is not drafted yet and landed the moment it is,
  with no new look; its questions as cards in the conversation.
- **Skip and Stop:** `POST /api/agents/{id}/stop` ends the model run in the agent's thread
  (`LIVE.stop`), and for Sherlock records the person's binding decision "No research for …";
  the workflow card says "Sherlock is looking into how others do this; a deeper look takes
  longer · Skip" while a look is open (`research` on the card's workflow); the agent page has
  Stop while running. A fourth run verdict, **stopped** ("Stopped" in the window), for a run
  the person ended.
- **`@Sherlock <question>`:** a full look, the gist in the conversation, the page kept.
- **Its notes,** code-written: per kind of work, how many suggestions the checks kept and
  dropped and which sites they came from.
- The journey suite's `workflow` check takes `sherlock_min`; the journey
  `sherlock_looks_into_it`.
