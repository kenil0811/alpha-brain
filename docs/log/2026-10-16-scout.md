## What was built

- **`runtime/agents/scout.py`** (the squirrel). **When:** the window's first Home poll of a
  local day leaves one `saw` line, "Alpha opened for the day." (`note_opened`, a preference
  remembers the day); Scout's look wakes on it once per day; `@Scout` and Run now pick now.
  Nothing runs when data arrives: what arrived is in the day's packet.
- **The packet, by code** (`packet`, `_words`): commitments due within a week, the calendar's
  next 48 hours, each table this week against last from the module summaries, asks and
  proposals left open for more than a day, sentences of the same shape asked three times or
  more this month (`_repeated_asks`), the active goals, suggested facts waiting, the picks the
  person declined before and the kinds they keep declining. The map's links pass
  (`connecting.connect`) runs first, by code; its kept links become one pick that opens the map.
- **The judgement:** one turn with Alpha's read tools (`scout_picks.md`): picks of four kinds
  (coming up, a pattern, a follow-up, by hand) with a subject, evidence ids, one action in the
  person's words, how sure, how helpful, costly to miss, when it expires, the goal it serves.
- **What code keeps** (`check_picks`): a known kind and a title; exactly one action (a
  semicolon or "and then" is two); evidence that resolves (journal, record, goal, commitment,
  fact, entity, calendar event, or table/id and page); not declined before with the same
  evidence (declines are the person's decisions keyed by kind and subject, with the evidence
  kept; `declined_before`); a kind declined `sinks_after` times running shows only its sure
  picks (`sunk_kinds`; a take resets); goal-serving first, then the surest; at most
  `picks_a_day`. Both numbers in `limits.toml` with their why; `less_sure` under 0.6, `card`
  when sure and costly.
- **Where it speaks:** the picks are `proposed` entries under Scout's name with `data.scout`;
  Home gets the day's undecided ones (`picks`, "Scout's picks" with Take and Not now, the kind
  or "Less sure" as the badge, "On a Take: …"); `needs_you` skips them unless `card`, so the
  companion's next poll shows only the sure, costly one; a goal's page lists the picks serving
  it. **Take** runs through the proposals route: the person's decision (`decide_pick`, by key,
  taken or not, journaled "You took / left Scout's pick: …"), then Alpha as a turn "Yes, go
  ahead: <action>" (the interview follows for a thing to stop doing by hand, Sherlock after it),
  or the map opens for a links pick; **Not now** is the decision alone.
- **Memory:** notes per kind, code-written (shown counts); the verdict succeeded when picks came
  back and passed the checks, or when it said plainly that nothing was worth attention.
- The journey suite's `picks` check; the journey `scout_picks_the_day`.
