# The split: two giants into packages, three rules made one (3 October 2026, night)

Kenil: "ok lets do 1,2,3" (the pending list's split, fast turns and the desktop's four). This
entry is the first. The checkpoint had found two files holding 2,300 of 11,000 core lines
(`mcp/tools.py` at 1,279, `api/server.py` at 1,252), "same site" computed in two places with
two answers, a name rule written four times, and a proposal answered in seven places by hand.

**The tools** are a package, `mcp/tools/`, one module per thing they touch, each a class on a
shared base: `finding` (search, the journal), `tables` (making tables in a build, rows, saved
lists, the one-row log), `knowledge` (pages, instructions, goals, facts, people), `modules`
(modules, threads, asking and proposing), `reading` (folders, documents, pages, readers, skills,
sign-ins, the calendar, sources), `automating`, `acting`, `planning`; `base` holds the `@tool`
decorator, the provenance words and the gates; `Tools` is all of them on one World and `all()`
still lists them in name order, so the MCP server hands the model the same 71 tools. Every method
was copied verbatim (an `ast` walk by name, asserted complete); only imports moved.

**The API** is a package, `api/`: `bodies` (what the window sends), `turns` (the background
turns), `views` (what the window shows, shaped from the world), `served` (what every route
closes over: the world, the scheduler, the turns in flight, the runner, `live`, the guard), and
one module of routes per area (`home`, `tables`, `people`, `intelligence`, `actions`,
`connections`, `settings`, `conversation`), each a `routes(app, served)` whose closures are the
old ones unchanged. `server.py` keeps `create_app` (the lifespan, CORS, the guard, health, then
the mounts) and `serve`. One smell went with it: `action_view` read the world from a module
global; it takes the world now.

**Three rules, one place each.** A site is `world/sites.py`'s `site_of` (the registrable domain:
linkedin.com, example.co.uk); the browser, readers, procedures, the purge and sources all use it,
where sources had their own host-only version (so a new source's `site` is now `example.co.uk`,
not `jobs.example.co.uk`; a source whose address isn't one keeps its words). A name is
`world/names.py`'s `check_name` (lower-case letters, digits, underscores, a letter first, 48 at
most), said one way for tables, fields, readers, skills and procedures (readers, skills and
procedures used to need two characters; one now does, as tables always could). A proposal is
answered by `journal.answer_proposal` (Yes or No, or other words: a standing permission, a
revised plan), with what it was about, in the seven places that wrote the entry by hand.
