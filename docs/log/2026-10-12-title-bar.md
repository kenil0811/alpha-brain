# The sidebar's top row is the Mac title bar (12 Oct 2026)

Branch `feat/title-bar`, off `feat/ui-rulebook` at c0d4f6e. The owner allowed the change under
`desktop/src-tauri/` on 12 Oct (`core-changes-for-the-window.md`, "Not adopted").

## What was built

- **Host:** the main window has `titleBarStyle: "Overlay"`, `hiddenTitle` and the window
  controls at (18, 26), centred on the 52px header line. No new permission
  (`allow-start-dragging` was already granted).
- **Window** (`shell/titleBar.ts`; only in the app on a Mac, so a browser is unchanged):
  - one listener drags the window from any empty part of the header line (the sidebar's top
    row, every page's header); a control, anything focusable, a right-click or a ctrl-click
    never drags;
  - a double-click is left to macOS (zoom or minimize, the person's setting). A first version
    also zoomed from the page, so macOS and the page zoomed twice and nothing seemed to happen;
  - room for the controls (`--window-controls-w`) before the workspace; folded, the top row is
    all controls and the workspace sits just below; in full screen the room goes
    (`titlebar--full`, from the window's resize events);
  - on the Mac the sidebar's narrowest width is 220 (was 160), so the workspace's name keeps
    room beside the controls;
  - header text isn't selectable; the folded panels' double-click and the drag share one list of
    what counts as a control.
- **Rulebook:** §3's title bar line, with how it behaves.

## The review

An agent that did not build it found no blockers and six issues:
- full screen kept the room;
- the narrowest sidebar lost the name;
- a quick second press didn't drag;
- ctrl-click dragged;
- a dead `.assist--folded` selector;
- double-clicking the folded row could zoom and unfold at once.

All six were fixed:
- the folded row is now plain title bar;
- the list of controls was widened and shared;
- the drag's import is made once;
- tests were added for the controls, right- and ctrl-clicks, full screen, and the folded
  sidebar's layout.

Not done: the native parts (dimmed controls when inactive) follow the Mac's light or dark, not
Settings › Appearance.
