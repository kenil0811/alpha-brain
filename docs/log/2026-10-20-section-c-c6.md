The spec: the desk's context holds outcomes as paragraphs only; "what exactly did you do
there" is the desk querying the `journal` view by the work's thread; work finished more than a
day ago is never in the desk's context nor the Now strip; the desk compacts like any session,
with its checkpoint.

## What the code did already

- **The roster is gone.** B3's WORK ITEMS section went with C1–C3. In its place, "WORK BESIDE
  YOU" listed the desk's work from the last day, one line each, cut at 160 characters.
- **The Now strip** shows only work that is running or waiting on the person.
- **The desk keeps the engine's compaction hooks** (what to keep, the start layer again after
  it). Home's checkpoint is written afresh after a compaction in one of the person's turns,
  apart from the desk (C1–C3's fix).

## Built

- **The per-turn layer at the desk** (`context/layers.py`):
  - **work running:** one line each, with its thread and task ids and its last line (a
    message reaches it by the task's id);
  - **work finished since the desk's last turn:** its outcome whole, as a paragraph. "Since"
    is by the order lines were written, not by their second, so an outcome and a reply in the
    same second keep their order. A piece stopped with no outcome gets its last line;
  - **work finished earlier in the last day:** one line, with its thread. The desk told the
    person its outcome in its own turn, which its session holds;
  - **older than a day:** nothing.
- **Nothing of a work's own journal at the desk**, even as news or as a match:
  - the "since your last turn here" lines leave out the work's threads;
  - the journal matches for the sentence leave them out in SQL (`journal.search`'s
    `not_threads`).

  Before, a step or a write inside the work could reach the desk as "elsewhere, meanwhile" or
  as a match.
- **`world_query`'s words:** what a piece of work beside you did, step by step, and its
  outcome is one query on the `journal` view by its `thread`.
- **A compaction in a turn the desk takes on finished work** (`runtime/desk.py`):
  - **the gap:** no sentence of the person's is in flight in such a turn, so the hooks stood
    in with an empty request. The compaction was told what to keep, but the start layer was
    not given again, and Home's checkpoint was not written afresh;
  - **the fix:** such a turn carries its own request. Its start layer is made only if a
    compaction asks for it. After the turn, Home's checkpoint is written apart from the
    desk's session, on a thread of its own, never blocking the desk's reader;
  - **seen only in a test:** a long-lived desk compacts more often than a per-turn session
    did, but this path has not been seen in a real run.
- **A work's steps are its calls** (`runtime/desk.py`, found by the journey below):
  - **the gap:** the C1–C3 proposal said the hook journals each of a subagent's calls under
    the work's thread. What was built journaled the SDK's progress messages instead, and in a
    real run their description is the task's own title. So a work's journal held its brief,
    one line repeating its title, and its outcome: nothing step by step to read back;
  - **the fix:** every call the work makes is now journaled from PreToolUse under its thread,
    in the words the window shows ("Reading the tables: SELECT …", "Running: …"), with the
    tool's name. A tool search is left out, since that is the harness finding a tool, not a
    step;
  - a progress message only keeps the work marked running;
  - the window's "Show the work" fold reads these lines, so it now shows the work's calls.
- **The queries:** both journal lookups use the thread's index (query plan checked on his
  world; `+kind` keeps the planner off the kind's index, which would scan every reply). The
  threads table is scanned: 32 rows in his world.
