## What was built

### The core
- **The table.** `world/tasks.py` keeps `alpha_tasks`, titled Tasks, owned by no module, marked
  as the world's own (`world_own` → `schema.world`).
  - It is named so it can never be a person's own "tasks" table. The model reads it as the
    view `tasks` in `world_query`.
  - Fields: title, state (Open · Working · Blocked · Done · Dropped), due, priority, owner,
    anchor ("Where it lives"), about (a relation holding many, "any" target), parent,
    blocked_by, repeats, remind_at, reminded_at, by, suggested.
  - Its first lists: Open (the default), Today, This week, Overdue, By place (the tree),
    Agents', Blocked, Waiting on others, Suggested.
  - It stays off Home's loose tables and off the work map.
- **A per-table hook in `Collections`.** `TableHook.shape` runs before a write: defaults, the
  owner and place checked, a repeat rule Alpha can't read refused. `TableHook.changed` runs inside
  the write's transaction: a repeating task moved to Done makes its next one there. Removal
  listeners mean a record removed takes the tasks living on it and its fields.
- **Commitments grew into tasks.**
  - On open, each `commitments` row becomes a task once, with its dates, state, owner and the
    side it is owed to kept. The old table and its index are dropped, as the skills table was
    in 2b. Watson's write list says `tasks`.
  - All 46 references moved over: the Reminders agent, the reminder card's Done and Tomorrow,
    Watson's filing (a task at once, "from what you said", Q91 #4), the pre-pack's TASKS DUE,
    Scout's packet, a person's page, the purge and the folded "Watson kept" line.
- **Places.**
  - An anchor is a window address: `#/m/<id>`, `…/<table>/<record>`, `…/<record>/<field>`,
    `#/m/<id>/~page/<name>` or `#/records/<table>/<record>[/<field>]`.
  - It is checked against the world and kept by id. `place()` gives the breadcrumb in words.
  - The model places a task with `world_remember kind="task"` (`place`: `table/id[/field]`,
    `page:<name>`, a module; left out, the conversation's module, else none) and says in one
    line where it put it (Q87).
- **Suggested tasks.** What a custom agent's run makes is suggested (Q91 #3): the tools'
  provenance carries `agent` in a run. Watson's tasks are not suggested.
- **Around it.** `Tasks.around()` returns, nearest first:
  - the place's values with their sources (and kind and unit);
  - the parent, the children and what blocks it;
  - the tasks beside it on the same place, and what it is about;
  - the module's notes.

  The model gets the same text by `world_remember kind="task"` with the id alone.
- **Routes.** `/api/tasks?at=`, `/api/tasks/{id}/around`, `POST /api/tasks`, `…/keep`,
  `…/drop` and `…/place` (an address, or a module, table, record and field).
- **Removal.** A module deleted for good takes the tasks living in it, and its plan says how
  many. A removal card says "1 open task on it goes too." A person removed takes the tasks they
  owned and leaves the others' About.
- **Two generic fixes on the way:**
  - `world_query`'s per-table views quote their columns: a field named like a word of SQL
    ("references", "order") broke every query.
  - Saved lists keep their order (`created_at, rowid`).

### The window
- **The Tasks page.** Tasks in the rail under People & Companies (`#/tasks`). It is the
  table's own page, so every view, edit and list works there.
- **Generic additions to the table page:**
  - a **Tree** view: places nested by their words, rows under the row they are part of, a
    nested row saying the field it lives on, a place's name opening it;
  - **relative dates** in filters: is today, is this week, is before today, in the next N days;
  - a **relation holding many** (pills);
  - **words in place of refs** (a task's place, owner, maker);
  - a **scope** prop (a part of a table, with the values a new row starts with).
- **A record's page** has a **Tasks** section: the tasks on it and its fields, Show done, and
  Add task here with a field choice.
- **A module's page** has a **Tasks** tab: its tasks, with their own remembered view.
- **A task's page** is a record page plus:
  - the suggestion with Keep and Drop, at the top;
  - **Where it lives**, a breadcrumb that opens each step, with Change (find a row or a
    module, or no place);
  - **Around it**, with Add a step under it.
- **Other pages:** a person's page lists their tasks. The Intelligence Commitments card is gone.
  Watson's folded line says tasks.

## Where it differs from the proposal

- **The table's name** is `alpha_tasks`, read as `tasks`. "tasks" could already be a person's
  table, and table names are global.
- **"References" is the field `about`.** "references" is a word of SQL.
- **"No new hand"** holds, but `world_remember` gained a `task` kind. Placing a task needs the
  place said the model's way, built into an address by code.
- **"Around it in front of any turn about the task"** is on demand (the kind with the id). The
  core has no page focus to know a turn is about a task; P2 puts it in front of a run that serves
  one.
- **Added:** a `blocked_by` field ("the tasks blocking it" needed somewhere to live).
