## A second spike, before building

Four things the desk's design depended on, tried on the SDK the same way:
- **(d) and (e):** a query sent while work runs is a turn of its own, with its own result; the
  subagent's messages interleave with it (their `parent_tool_use_id` set).
- **(f):** the turn Claude Code takes on finished work fires UserPromptSubmit, with the
  `<task-notification>` (its task ids) as the prompt. So each turn of the session, the
  person's or the desk's own, begins at a known point and ends with its result, in order.
- **(g):** an in-process hand call carries `claudecode/toolUseId` in its `_meta`. The
  permission callback's context carries `agent_id` and `tool_use_id`. So every call can be
  bound to the thread it serves.

## C1: the desk

- **`runtime/desk.py`, new.** One desk per world, on a thread and event loop of its own. It
  holds one `ClaudeSDKClient` resumed on Home's session for the core's life, with Alpha's
  hands, hooks and permission callback.
  - A person's sentence is a query into it (`Desk.run`, a `turn.Runner`). One reader follows
    the whole stream and settles the session's turns in the order they began.
  - The start layer goes with a session's first sentence only, and the per-turn layer with
    every one.
  - A change of hands, skills or how the world is given reconnects it on the same session,
    but only when no work runs.
  - A turn silent past the silence limit drops the client, and the next sentence connects
    afresh.
- **Every sentence is the desk's** (`api/turns.py`). No routing judgement runs before a
  sentence. Two cases stay by structure: an `@Name` reaches that agent in its own item, and a
  sentence for an agent's own thread stays its (A10). An old item's box, a page's button and
  Alpha's own turns (a file read, a yes on a card) all go to the desk.
  - A page's button no longer opens a page item (`pages.thread_of` is gone). Its words go to
    the desk with the page's module.
- **One desk for every module** (Q107, question 2):
  - the desk's directory links every module's skills, the first of a name winning, with a
    clash noted in the log;
  - it reaches every module's folder;
  - its sandbox allows every module's listed hosts;
  - a sentence from a module's page brings the module and its notes in the per-turn layer
    ("THIS SENTENCE CAME FROM A MODULE'S PAGE"). The desk's start layer is the same whichever
    page the first sentence came from.
- **B1's paragraph 6** is Kenil's sentence: "You are the one voice; work you start runs beside
  you and reports back to you; you tell the person what happened in your own words."
- **The Home roster ("WORK ITEMS") is gone from the per-turn layer.** "WORK BESIDE YOU" takes
  its place: running work with its task's id (for `SendMessage`), and work that finished in the
  last day, one line each (C6's part).
- **Tests never reach the real model through the desk.** A conftest fixture stands a desk with
  no model in for `desk.of`. An API test answered a card with no fake runner and had started
  a real client. Before C, the same path already reached `route.run`.

## C2: work beside the desk

- **The desk's own `Agent` call runs in the background.** S2's foreground hook is now only for
  runs.
- **Each task the desk starts** (its `TaskStartedMessage`, matched to the Agent call by its id)
  becomes:
  - a thread of kind `work`, with `session_tools` `desk-work` and `session_ref` the task's id;
  - a line in Home, "Started work beside the conversation: <title>", carrying the thread's id;
  - its brief ("Asked to: …") on its own thread.
- **Its steps:** each `TaskProgressMessage` is its last line and a step line on its thread.
- **Its calls:** each call it makes is bound to its thread, through the hook's `agent_id` and
  the hand's `toolUseId`. So what it writes is journaled there, and its cards say so.
- **When it ends** (`TaskNotificationMessage`), its thread is settled and its last message is
  kept there as its outcome. The desk's own turn on it is journaled in Home, "Work finished:
  <title>", then its reply, which carries the thread's id.
- **A shell in a subagent always runs in the foreground** (the spike's early completions).
- **Stop on a piece of work** is the SDK's `stop_task` (`POST /api/work/{id}/stop`).
- **Work left running when the core closed** is stopped when it starts again, with "Alpha
  closed while this ran, so it stopped. Try again to start it afresh."

## C3: the stream, in the window

- **`GET /api/conversation` is the stream.** It holds Home's sentences and replies, and the
  person's conversations from before C as history. Its only `did` lines are where work
  started. It names the work it shows (`work`), the work running now (`now`), every open
  question wherever it arose, the proposed actions and pages, and the other waiting cards
  (staged rows, pairs, suggestions, facts: `needs`).
  - `module` narrows it to a module's page.
  - `work` narrows it to one piece of work, with the sentence that started it.
  - An agent's own thread, named, is still its own (an agent's page).
  - An `@Name` and the agent's answer are in the stream, the answer marked "<Name> answers"
    (Q107, question 3); both still live in the agent's own thread.
- **The window** (`Conversation`, `assistant/Work.tsx`):
  - the person's sentences and the desk's replies, in order;
  - work as one live message (its chip, state and last step, Stop, "Show the work"), which
    gives way to the desk's reply on it. That reply carries the work's chip and its fold, and
    the fold lists its steps from its thread's journal with no model call;
  - a chip filters the stream ("Only: … · Show everything");
  - the Now strip above the composer: the running work, three at most and "+N", each a
    filter, with a dot and a count when something waits on the person;
  - every card inline at its time: questions, actions, pages, and Home's other cards through
    Home's own `Need`.
- **Gone from the window:** the conversation picker and + New, the item cards ("Sent to …",
  open), an item's own box, the Assistant page's lanes and list (the page is the stream at
  full height), Home's "Waiting on you" item cards, and rename/delete of conversations
  (`ConversationActions`).
  - Home's Needs-you cards became one line, "N things wait on you, in the conversation", with
    "Open the conversation". The cards are in the stream.
- **The companion** shows the stream's tail (sentences and replies) and a Now line. Its box
  goes to the desk, and the "Which is this about?" pills are gone.

## Found by the journeys, fixed

- **A slash command at the desk never got its result.** `/compact` begins its turn with no
  UserPromptSubmit, so the reader took its result for nobody's, and the turn waited out the
  silence limit twice (`compaction_keeps_what_was_made`'s compact step, 1,232 s, "not ok",
  though its checks passed).

- **A removal's deferral never resumes in a kept session.** The yes began a new turn, and the
  model asked again on a second card (`removal_waits_on_its_card`).
  - A deferred call resumes only when a new process takes the session up (P3 item 7). So at
    the desk a removal waits on its card while the turn stays open, as a host's card and a
    helper's removal already do, and the yes lets it through in the same turn.
  - The journey's words and its answered check follow this.
  - What this gives up: a removal card no longer outlives a restart at the desk. Answered
    after one, its yes is a new sentence.
- **A side call went into the desk's session.** After a compaction, Watson's checkpoint is
  written with the turn's runner, and with the desk as that runner its prompt became a
  sentence of the conversation (found by a test that failed only after the conversation
  tests had loaded Watson).
  - Now `Desk.run` takes only the conversation's own turns. A judge, a checkpoint or a
    second opinion runs apart, as before C.
- **The compaction's own hooks found no turn.** `/compact`'s PreCompact and SessionStart run
  before any message of its turn, so the guidance and the start layer given again were lost.
  - Now the desk's turn is also the sentence sent whose turn has not begun.
- **A yes asked mid-turn was not drawn in the stream while the turn ran.** Home's Needs-you
  list used to show it, and it is now one line. The stream shows a permission card while the
  turn waits on it, and other questions after the turn, as before. A window test covers it.
