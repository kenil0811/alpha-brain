desktop in parallel; the two conflicted in 29 files. Vikas chose main's kit: its components, table
views, rig, Skill and Automation pages, ⌘K, map and addresses stay, and his behaviours were moved
on top. Branch `feat/zazoo-ui`. UI only: nothing under `core/` or `connectors/`; under
`desktop/src-tauri/` only the panda icons and the tray's `iconPath`.

**Coming soon, one way.** `ui/Soon.tsx`: a control whose route or host command doesn't exist yet

control and the route it waits for is in `docs/development/backend-requests.md`.

**Zazoo.** The assistant is called Zazoo in everything a person reads (panel, composer, ⌘K,
companion, Intelligence, modules, Settings); Alpha stays the app's name.

images, a folder or audio through `/api/files`; per-chat model and access say coming soon. New
chat in the panel's header. Double click edits a person's page and an action's text.

character, or in another app closes it. Light, contact shadow, eye highlights, blink squash,

icons. Its default place stays the host's bottom-right corner.
