# Watson, the first built-in agent (15 October 2026)

Q46 built on Q50's substrate, proposed in writing and agreed ("yes to all" to seven questions:
the refiling by itself with a journal line each, commitments as a table, decisions stated in
conversation as binding, the checkpoint when a conversation closes, documents yes and emails
not yet, the koala until the owl is drawn, Haiku for the passes).

## What was built

- **`runtime/agents/watson.py`** replaces `runtime/noticing.py`. The kind registers its `look`
  and `run`. `look` reads the journal from Watson's cursor: the person's turns that have a
  reply (worth a pass: more than a yes, four words or more), a conversation that closed or went
  quiet, a document Alpha read or the person added; readers' rows are never its business. The
  cursor stops short of a turn still waiting for its reply, so nothing is missed. `run` does the
  passes and judges the verdict by code: succeeded, partial (an answer missing for some), failed
  (none); its line "Kept 2 facts, 1 page lines; 1 to confirm".
- **Five item types, filed by code.** A fact about the person (their page) or about a person or
  company (theirs; never from a first name alone, never when two share a name); a page line; a
  commitment (who, what, to whom, by when); a decision the person reached (recorded as theirs,
  binding, with their words as evidence, about the conversation and its module); and "other"
  (a request, a setting, a passing state), which is filed nowhere. What the person states is
  accepted; what Watson infers waits as a suggestion. The `noticed` trail names what went where.
- **A conversation's checkpoint** (`checkpoint`): the decisions it reached and one "what is
  open" line under "Open" on the module's page (the person's Profile for General).
- **A document** (`read_document`): the same pass over its text for the people and companies in
  it, with nothing stated about the person themselves.
- **Commitments:** `world/commitments.py` and the `commitments` table (who, what, to whom, due,
  state open/done/lapsed, the turn); on the person's page (Intelligence › Knowledge) and a
  person's page; the pre-pack lists what is due within a week (COMMITMENTS DUE); purge takes a
  person's with them.
- **Decisions** stated in conversation go into `decisions` (slice A) and show on the module's
  page under "Decisions".
- **The refiling, once** (`refile`): the facts kept about the person before Watson, each judged
  by the turn's own model (not the small one: Haiku called the person's weight and job search
  "passing state" in the first try) against an explicit keep list, moved by code: a setting, a
  request or a passing state is forgotten as a fact with a journal line saying why and where it
  lives; recorded as Watson's decision so it never repeats. On a copy of Kenil's world the
  stronger model moved exactly the 11 that are not about him (five workflow settings, four
  requests, two states) and kept the 13 that are.
- **`runtime/agents/builtin.py`**: the built-in kinds and `ensure(world)`, called when the core
  starts (`create_app`) and by the journey suite: the row made once, its brief page from the
  kind's default, its cursor after its own hello line.
- **The substrate used for real:** `Turns.notice` is gone; the scheduler's kick after a turn
  runs `looks()`, and Watson is the first kind it wakes. The journey suite runs
  `look_and_run(world, "watson")` after each say, as the app's kick would. `WRITES` gained
  `decisions` and `commitments`.
- **Window:** `Commitment` and `ModuleDetail.decisions`; a Commitments card on the person's page
  and section on a person's; Decisions on a module's page; a built-in kind's own face until the
  person picks one (`KIND_LOOKS`: Watson the koala); the Agents tab and the agent page say so.
- **The journey judge** sees what the model was given about whoever the sentence names (the
  pre-pack section kept with the turn): `memory_noticing`'s judge had called Vikas's move to
  Bangalore, kept by Watson the turn before, invented, because memory leaves no step in the
  turn that uses it.

## Proven

275 core tests (`test_watson.py`: the eight of noticing, now Watson's, plus the five types, the
pre-pack line, the checkpoint, a document, the cursor and the run, the refiling); 199 desktop
tests; ruff, mypy and typecheck clean; `just check-desktop` 95 of 95 pages clean, Watson's page
among them (`docs/checks/2026-10-15-1649.md`). Journeys with the real model on a copy of
Kenil's world: `memory_follow_up` and `memory_two_sources` passed (the latter had failed on
14 Oct), `memory_who_emailed` still fails as on 14 Oct (Alpha answers from memory instead of
opening Gmail; pending, not Watson's), and `memory_noticing` passed on its third run
(`docs/journeys/2026-10-15-1701.md`): the first two runs gave the right answer and the judge
called it invented, which is what the evidence fix above is for. The kept copy of that run
shows Watson working with the real model: two runs, both succeeded; on Vikas's page a line
and two facts (relocation_plan, cofounder_interest); the second turn's card carried them;
the refiling's journal lines.
