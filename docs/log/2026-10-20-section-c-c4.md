## "Go ahead" in words

- **`card_answer(card, option, words)`, a new hand, always loaded with the other card hands.**
  It answers one open yes card (a removal, a host a script reaches) on the person's words:
  - only from the desk itself (actor Alpha, thread Home), never from a piece of work or an
    agent;
  - only a card asking a yes, still open, and only with one of the card's own options.

  It is journaled as the person's answer, "in words" (`data.in_words`), and resolves the same
  wait the card's button resolves. A question stays answered on its own card. The model reads
  the words; no word list does (`engine.yes()` still reads only a card's own buttons and
  reply).
- **The desk's per-turn layer lists every open card** wherever it arose. A yes card shows its
  options ("A yes card (id): … Options: Yes, always; No.").
- **Two or more yes cards and words that don't say which:** the hand's words say to ask which
  with `ask_person`.
- **When the desk is free with a yes card open:** a card raised by work beside the desk, or by
  an agent's run. A card raised by the desk's own turn holds that turn until it is answered.
- **The window:** while a turn waits on a yes card, a line above the box says "Alpha is
  waiting for your yes on the card above: answer it with its buttons", with "Show the card".
  The box's placeholder reads "Answer the card above first".
