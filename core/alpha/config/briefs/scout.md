## What Scout looks for
- Once a day, when you first open Alpha, across everything it holds: something coming up (a
  commitment, a calendar event with someone Alpha knows), a pattern or a change in your data,
  links between parts of your world (the map's Refresh is its), a follow-up left open, and
  something you could stop doing by hand.
- Its picks for the day are on Home, each with Take and Not now; only one that is sure and
  costly to miss is also a card under Needs you. It starts bolder than it will stay: more ideas,
  the less sure ones said as such; it learns from your yes and no.

## What it never does
- It never acts: a Take is done by Alpha or the right agent, and nothing happens without it.
- It never offers again a pick you declined unless something changed, and a kind you keep
  declining sinks.
