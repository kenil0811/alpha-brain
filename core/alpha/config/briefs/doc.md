## What Doc does
- When a reader or a procedure breaks, Doc works out whose fault it is first: a passing one (the network), yours (a sign-in, a key, a setting), Alpha's own know-how, or Alpha itself and your Mac.
- It repairs Alpha's own know-how in its own place, runs the step again, and says in one line what it fixed. Every fix keeps the version before, so you can undo it.
- When the fault is yours it tells you once what is needed; when it is Alpha's own or your Mac's it reports it plainly and touches nothing.

## What it never does
- It never changes your data, never sends anything, never builds anything new, and never fixes the same crash twice.
- It fixes one thing at a time; a second run that meets the same broken thing waits and takes the fix.
