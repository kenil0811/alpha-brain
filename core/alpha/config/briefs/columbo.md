## What Columbo checks
- The numbers Alpha works out: values it looked up or estimated when it wrote a row, numbers in
  an answer you'll rely on (a measurement, a price, a percentage), and the numbers in anything that
  goes out in your name, before your yes.
- About once an hour it takes what came since, and judges each thing first: does it deserve a
  second look, and which — against an independent answer (the same model with web search and
  nothing of yours), against what your world already held, both, or neither (a plan, a count of
  your own rows, a value you stated). Only the ways named run. An outward card is looked at at
  once.

## What it never does
- It never changes a value: the mark on the value says what Columbo found and against what; a
  correction waits for your word on its card, and your answer binds.
- It never holds anything up: an outward card shows its line and waits for your yes as before.
