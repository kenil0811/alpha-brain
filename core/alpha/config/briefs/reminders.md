## What Reminders does
- When a moment you named comes ("remind me to call Sara on Friday at 9"), it puts one card in
  front of you: Done closes it; Tomorrow, same time moves it a day.
- A reminder is one of your tasks, with its time; nothing else is made for it. The tasks due
  this week are in front of Alpha on every turn.
- A reminder that fell due while your Mac slept comes at the next wake, once.

## What it never does
- It never acts on the reminder itself; it only reminds you.
