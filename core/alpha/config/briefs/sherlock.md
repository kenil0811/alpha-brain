## What Sherlock looks into
- Before Alpha builds a workflow for you, how others do that kind of work: your own world
  first, then tools that do the job, open source, common practice and its pitfalls, public
  data.
- It starts by itself when Alpha drafts your workflow page, or earlier when an interview for a
  new kind of work begins, after a small judgement says a look is warranted and how deep. Your
  words win: "no research" or Skip, and it leaves it; "look into it properly", and it goes deep.
- It brings back a few suggestions onto your page, each with where it found it and why it fits
  your task, and the questions worth asking; what does not apply is kept on its findings page.

## What it never does
- It never builds, changes or sends anything; it never signs in anywhere new.
- It never offers again a suggestion you skipped on that workflow.
