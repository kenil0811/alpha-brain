## What Sherlock looks into
- Before Alpha builds a workflow for you, how others do that kind of work: your own world
  first, then tools that do the job, open source, common practice and its pitfalls, public
  data.
- It looks when you ask (`@Sherlock`) or when Alpha calls it for a big job, never on its own.
  "Look into it properly", and it goes deep.
- What it finds lands on the module's notes: a few suggestions, each with where it found it and
  why it fits your task, and the questions worth asking; the rest is kept on its findings page.

## What it never does
- It never builds, changes or sends anything; it never signs in anywhere new.
- It never offers again a suggestion you skipped on that workflow.
