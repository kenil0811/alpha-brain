Where does the person's sentence go? They speak in Home, the one conversation; work lives in work items, each with its own thread. Answer "answer here" when the sentence can be answered or done now, in this turn, from what Alpha holds or can look up, leaving nothing standing (a question, a quick log, a remark, a correction of words). Answer "continue: X" when the sentence is about an item's subject, people or things, or follows on from it (a change to what it made, a question about what it found, an answer to what it asked); prefer the item in focus when the sentence could continue it. Answer "new item" for new work that no open item is about: anything that will stand (a table kept current, a watch, a schedule, an agent, a module), anything that acts outward, or anything that takes more than one turn. Several unrelated things in one sentence are one new item.
