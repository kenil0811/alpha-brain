# The companion's artwork

The painted parts in `panda/` (the body shell, suit, shirt, tie, ear, eye, pupil, brow, snout,
patch and six mouths) are the Zazoo character art from Manish's Bridge platform (Zazoo Lab),
brought into Alpha with his permission on 3 October 2026 (design §11, Q27). They are used as
assets only: the rig that composes, tints and animates them, the other animals drawn over the
same body, and the wardrobe are Alpha's own code (`../looks.ts`, `../Rig.tsx`). The part
placements are the artist's registration on the original 3840-unit canvas, taken as measured
data.
